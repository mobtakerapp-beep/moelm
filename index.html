Short forms (اضغط لتسمع)

We’re = We are → We’re wearing pyjamas.

They’re = They are → They’re wearing jeans.

You’re = You are → You’re wearing a coat.

Our – Their – Your

Our → Our pyjamas are pink.

Their → Their baseball cap is blue.

Your → Your trainers are red.
